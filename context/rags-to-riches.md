# Rags to Riches Cleaning (Nita's business)

> Satellite page. Not Theo's own business, he builds the tech for his sister Nita, who owns and runs it. Uncle T Agency is the main focus of this workspace; this gets a lighter touch until it's Nita's turn in the priority queue.

Nita juggles up to 20 clients at once. Real pricing, from her paper invoice book: Basic Clean, R420, and Linen change / wash and dry, R196, billed as separate line items per visit.

Theo already built her a branded HTML invoicing app (her colors, pink/red, #c1185a), with editable fields, auto-calculating totals, and a "Send Invoice" button that turns the invoice into an image and pushes it straight into WhatsApp's share sheet. A "Save as PDF" button covers record-keeping. It's set up as an installable PWA.

**Next in the queue, after CHALES:** a Make.com batch automation that reads her Google Sheet, generates PDFs for every unsent invoice, and sends them automatically, a blueprint already exists for this. After that: a cleaning website, and fixing her bookkeeping (currently done manually by someone Theo's referred to once as "the Sara nightmare").

The longer-term idea: package this same invoicing setup as a sellable agency product for other small operators drowning in paper admin (cleaners, plumbers, gardeners, creches, handymen), roughly R750 to R1,200 once-off plus R149 to R399/month. Figures are directional, not locked.

Also on the list once CHALES is sorted: the same client-growth push Chante gets, more clients for Nita, not just better admin.
