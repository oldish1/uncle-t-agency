# The offer

## Products and services

**The Lexi System**, the flagship. A WhatsApp AI booking assistant that lives inside the client's own WhatsApp Business number. Greets the customer, lists services, takes date and time, checks live calendar availability so nothing double-books, confirms, and sends reminders to cut no-shows. Sold as "a receptionist who never sleeps and never double-books," never as "a chatbot" or "AI technology," the pitch leads with the pain (missed messages at night, no-shows, double bookings, the owner personally fielding WhatsApp between clients), not the tech.

Price: R1,499 once-off setup, R499/month recurring. Rollout: 4 days, build, connect, test, live.

**Websites.** A single mobile-first HTML site, built and hosted, R1,499 once-off with hosting and domain included in the R499/month. Positioned directly against Telecloud, who charge R2,000 to R11,000 once-off for the same tier of work and bill hosting separately.

**Not yet built, in the queue once Lexi is stable and there's real paying volume:** follow-up automation, Google review request automation, client reactivation campaigns. These are deliberately held back so no client is sold something unproven.

**Parked, not active:** an Android SMS gateway add-on (revisit once there are 5+ paying clients), an SA-adapted AI bookkeeping product (CSV bank exports through Make.com and the Claude API into Sheets and WhatsApp, multilingual, researched but not built).

## How it's sold

Direct outreach, WhatsApp and in-person, not paid ads. A 20-prospect Cape Town salon list runs through Google Sheets with a Make.com daily email automation on top, plus WhatsApp messages sent directly.

The strategy is five founding clients at a discounted rate in exchange for a testimonial and one trackable success metric each, banked as sales proof before any upsell gets offered. CHALES Hair Boutique is the live proof-of-concept client, referenceable and demoable right now.

Warm leads in the pipeline: Zanzibar Hair and Beauty (replied), Sistergirl (asked about pricing), Reeva Hair, JEM Hair and Beauty Studio, and Jason, a tattoo artist at No Love Lost, referred in through Theo's sister.


## Founding five deal (decided 25 Oct 2026)

For the first five paying salons: first 30 days free, then setup R999 (normal R1,499) and R499 a month. If she cancels in the first month she owes nothing. Estimated real cost to us per salon for the free month: R100 to R150 (see `plans/2026-10-25-game-plan-pricing-chales-google.md`).

**Positioning:** Lexi is the salon's personal assistant, not software. "Get found. Get booked. Get remembered." Competitors (Fresha, Booksy, Setmore) sell a tool the owner has to run; Lexi takes the WhatsApp work off the owner. Proof: Chales, about 100 real bookings since 10 June, about 34 clients.

**After the founders** (draft, not final): Starter R499 (Lexi bookings and reminders), Plus R799 (adds review requests, win-back messages, memberships), Grow R1,299 (adds Google profile care, monthly results report, website).
