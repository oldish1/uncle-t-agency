# The business

## What it is

Uncle T Agency is Theo's Cape Town digital agency. It builds and sells AI-powered WhatsApp automation, booking bots, and websites to small, local businesses, mostly salons, spas, and tradespeople who currently run bookings on paper or in their heads.

Money comes in two ways: a once-off setup fee when a system goes live, then a monthly recurring fee for keeping it running. The recurring piece is the actual business model. Theo has already walked away from a couple of other ideas (a forex bot, a plain "just build websites" pivot) specifically because they don't produce that kind of compounding monthly income.

## Who it serves

Small, local South African business owners, usually first- or second-generation, who take bookings by paper diary, memory, or personally answering WhatsApp at all hours. They lose money quietly: a missed call becomes a lost customer, a late reply loses the booking to whoever answered first.

They're based around Cape Town's Cape Flats-adjacent suburbs, Bellville, Mitchell's Plain, Parow, Goodwood. They'd never consider or afford a corporate agency's pricing. What they trust is a local operator who speaks Afrikaans, shows up in person, and reads as a neighbour rather than a salesman, which is exactly Theo's edge over corporate competitors like Telecloud.

Beyond hair salons, med spas, dog groomers, and cleaning businesses are current targets. Plumbers, dentists, and real estate agents are next. Hair braiders, home-based nail and lash techs, driving schools, custom cake bakers, mobile detailers and beauty therapists, appliance repair, and seamstresses are flagged as strong, mostly untapped verticals, because a missed booking costs those businesses more than most.

## How it runs

Solo operation. Theo owns sales, relationships, and strategy. Claude is the technical build partner, everything from the Make.com automations to the client sites gets built here.

Still a side venture against a full-time trade job (boilermaker and sheet metal work), built in evenings and weekends. Current stage: one live proof-of-concept client (CHALES Hair Boutique, his daughter Chante's salon), five warm leads in the pipeline, and a 20-prospect outreach list running. No paying agency client yet, that's deliberately gated behind fixing Lexi's response speed first.

What makes it different: pricing positioned well below corporate agencies (Telecloud charges R2,000 to R11,000 once-off for comparable work, with hosting extra; Uncle T Agency charges R1,499 once-off plus R499/month, hosting included), and a trust advantage no outside agency can match, being from the same community as the client.

## Competitors

Telecloud, cited above as the clearest price contrast, but the 27 Sept 2026 research run couldn't find any South African company by that name selling websites at R2,000 to R11,000. The only Telecloud found is a small Centurion phone/internet provider (AI Call Center from R1,250/month, an AI website builder at R200/month through sister company Lubb Systems), with no WhatsApp booking product at all. Where the R2,000 to R11,000 figure came from needs checking before it's used with a prospect again, see `outputs/deep-research/2026-09-27-salon-booking-pain-competitor-intel/`.

Also on the radar: Nextapt (a bare R1-per-booking WhatsApp button flow, no AI, no human help), WAFlowBot (runs on an unofficial WhatsApp connection through a third-party gateway, which risks the client's number getting banned), Chatty (chatty.co.za, a newly found local player at R499/month already doing review requests and win-back messages, closest price match to Lexi found so far), and BizAI, which also sells an AI phone receptionist ("Voice Valet"). Voice Valet's real current price (checked 27 Sept 2026) is a R999/month add-on ex-VAT on top of a separate R599/month CRM plan, only 100 minutes included then R3.50/minute, so the cheapest real entry is about R1,598/month and a busy salon at 800 minutes would pay close to R4,000/month. See `reference/research/2026-09-25-lexi-voice.md` for the earlier note and `outputs/deep-research/2026-09-27-salon-booking-pain-competitor-intel/02-bizai-voice-receptionist-sentiment.md` for the correction. None of them are positioned for Cape Flats-adjacent community businesses the way Uncle T Agency is, and none has any real independent customer reviews anywhere online, that's the gap being worked.
