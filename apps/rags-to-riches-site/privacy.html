Privacy Policy

Rags to Riches Cleaning · Cape Town · Last updated 1 October 2026

This page explains what information Rags to Riches Cleaning collects through its WhatsApp booking service, and how that information is used.

What we collect

When you book a clean through WhatsApp, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp number
  • Your name
  • Your apartment or address, building name, and access details (such as a lockbox code) that you choose to share so our cleaning team can get in
  • The service, date and time you book

How we use it

We use this information only to schedule and carry out your booking, to confirm details with you on WhatsApp, and to remember your details for future bookings so you don't have to repeat them. Access codes and addresses are shared only with the cleaning team assigned to your job.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your information with third parties. It's stored in our booking system (Google Sheets and Google Calendar) and used by Meta's WhatsApp Business API to send and receive your messages.

How long we keep it

We keep your booking details for as long as you're an active client, so returning bookings are quick. You can ask us to delete your details at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp.

Contact

Questions about your information? Message Rags to Riches Cleaning directly on WhatsApp.
