Questions

Do you bring your own cleaning products?

Yes, we bring everything we need.

Can I book a regular clean for every turnover?

Yes. Once you've booked once, the WhatsApp assistant remembers your apartment and access details, so rebooking takes a few taps.

How far ahead can I book?

You can pick any open day in the next 7 days. For dates further out, choose "Later date" and Nita will sort it out with you.

Do you work on Sundays and public holidays?

Yes. Sundays are R50 extra and public holidays R60 extra.

How do I pay?

By EFT. Nita sends the final price and banking details on WhatsApp.
