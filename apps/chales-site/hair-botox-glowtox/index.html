Questions people ask

Is hair botox the same as Botox injections?

No. There are no needles and nothing goes into your skin. It's a conditioning treatment applied to the hair, named for the way it fills and plumps damaged strands.

How much is hair botox in Cape Town?

At CHALES it's R1 200 for short to medium hair, R1 500 for long hair, and Glowtox is R1 400.

How long does hair botox last?

Usually 2 to 3 months, depending on how often you wash and heat-style your hair.

Hair botox or keratin?

Botox repairs and conditions while keeping your natural texture. Keratin is for smoothing and straightening. If your main problem is damage rather than frizz, botox is usually the place to start.

Can I have it on bleached hair?

Yes, bleached and coloured hair is often where it helps most. Tell Chanté what's been done recently so she can check your hair first.
