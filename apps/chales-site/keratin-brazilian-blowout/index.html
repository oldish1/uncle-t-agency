Questions people ask

How much is a keratin treatment in Cape Town?

At CHALES a keratin treatment or a Brazilian blowout is R1 500 for short to medium hair and R1 800 for long hair.

What's the difference between keratin and a Brazilian blowout?

A Brazilian blowout keeps more movement and body. Keratin gives a straighter, sleeker result and lasts a bit longer. Chanté will recommend one once she's seen your hair.

How long does a keratin treatment last?

Up to 3 to 4 months for keratin and about 10 to 12 weeks for a Brazilian blowout, depending on your hair and aftercare.

Should I colour before or after?

Colour first, smooth after is the usual order. Mention it when you book and Chanté will plan the two appointments.

Is there a formaldehyde-free option?

Yes. Nanoplastia is a formaldehyde-free smoothing treatment, also done at CHALES.
