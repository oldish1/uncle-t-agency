Questions people ask

How much are highlights in Cape Town?

At CHALES half head highlights are R650, full head highlights R950, and balayage R1 200 (R1 500 with a toner).

How often do I need a root touch-up?

Every 4 to 6 weeks for most people. A root touch-up is R450, or R650 with a blow dry.

Balayage or highlights?

Highlights are placed in fine, even sections for an all-over lift. Balayage is painted on by hand for a softer, grown-out look that needs fewer touch-ups.

Can I go much lighter in one visit?

It depends on your starting colour and your hair's condition. Chanté will tell you straight at your consultation, and may split it over two visits to protect your hair.

Can I colour and smooth my hair?

Yes. Colour first, then a smoothing treatment like Nanoplastia or keratin. Chanté can plan both.
