One workshop, many jobs.

Laser cutting

Flat parts cut straight from your drawing file or a sample. Brackets, plates, covers, panels and one-off shapes.

Press brake bending

Sheet folded to the angle on your drawing. Trays, channels, brackets and enclosures, one-off or repeated.

CNC punching

Hole patterns and shapes punched quickly and repeated exactly. A good fit when you need many of the same part.

Copper pot stills

New stills built for distilleries, and repairs when a condenser, column or pot needs work.

Supra meat machines

Bandsaws, mincers, mixers and sausage fillers. Spares for the full Supra range, plus repairs on other makes.

Also rolling, welding, turning, milling and general fabrication.
