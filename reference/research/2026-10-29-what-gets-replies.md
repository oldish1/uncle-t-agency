# What actually gets replies in cold outreach (evidence, 29 Oct 2026)

Sources: Sales.co (1.1M cold emails), Hunter.io (34M cold emails), Instantly 2026 benchmark, Woodpecker (20M+ emails, via Lemlist), GrowLeads (304K emails), SalesLoft via Vidyard (video), SBL (cold WhatsApp), Salesforge (timing, 7.5M emails), Ambition case study (578 prospects).

## Honest warning about this evidence

Almost all of it is cold **email** to **B2B** buyers, and most of it comes from companies that sell outreach tools. Data on cold WhatsApp to salon owners is thin. So "proven" here means "the same direction shows up across many sources". It is not a promise. We test it ourselves and keep our own numbers.

## What the data says

| Finding | Numbers |
|---|---|
| Normal cold reply rates are low | Cold email averages about 3.4% in 2026. True cold WhatsApp (no prior contact) runs about 1 to 5%. Warm WhatsApp (they opted in or wrote first) runs 20 to 40%. |
| Personal beats generic | No personalisation 1 to 3%; basic (name, business) 5 to 9%; specific detail about their business 9 to 15%, some reports up to 18%. |
| Short is a little better | Under 100 words: 3.9% against 3.7% for longer. The best band was 20 to 39 words. The effect is small. |
| One question beats a big ask | Asking a simple question beat asking for a meeting by about 42%. One call to action beat several by 35 to 42%. |
| Follow-ups matter | In 1.1M emails the second email got 87% as many replies as the first, and a quarter of all replies came from follow-ups. One to two weeks between emails did best (email data). An older case: 6 replies to the first send, 67 more from follow-ups. |
| Video helps | SalesLoft data reported by Vidyard: about 26% more replies and 16% more opens when video was added. |
| Timing | Tuesday to Thursday, 8 to 11 am the recipient's time. |

## What it means for us

1. Expect about 1 to 5 replies per 100 cold messages. Ten messages may give 0 to 2 replies. Volume plus follow-ups is the game, not one perfect message.
2. Our current drafts already do the proven things: one true detail about the salon, one named real client, one question, a video, an opt-out line. Do not rewrite them again.
3. Follow up. Day 3 and about day 10. Keep each under 50 words and add one new fact, never "just checking in".
4. Send the bulk Tuesday to Thursday mornings from next week.
5. Test our own variants (permission-first vs video attached) once we send 50 or more.

## Using the needle (no commission, flat price, clients stay yours, a real person answers)

Say it as fact, never as an attack. Only claim what we can point to. Fresha's own pricing page (checked 26 Oct 2026) shows a 20% one-off fee on new marketplace clients. Safe wording: "Some booking apps take a cut of new clients. Lexi is a flat R499 a month, no commission, and your clients stay in your own WhatsApp." Do not name a competitor in outreach. This is not legal advice; keep comparisons factual.
