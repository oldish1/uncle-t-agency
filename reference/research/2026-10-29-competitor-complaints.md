# What salon owners complain about (Fresha, Booksy, Setmore, Treatwell, Salonz)

Run 29 Oct 2026 with Firecrawl. Looking for the one thing competitors do badly that Lexi can do better.

## What was read, and what was not

Read: Trustpilot (Fresha, Booksy), Capterra and SoftwareAdvice (Fresha/Shedul), Setmore and Treatwell owner reviews, and review roundups that summarise Reddit threads (sorttheclicks.com, aasure.app, dothebeauty.com, pabau.com). Not readable: Reddit and Facebook groups directly (blocked), TikTok. South African products (Salonz, Zolmi) have almost no public reviews, which is itself a finding. This is a qualitative read, not a count of thousands of reviews.

## The themes, strongest first

1. **Fees nobody expected, and distrust.** Fresha: 20% one-off fee on new marketplace clients, and owners say they were charged for clients who found them on Google or by referral. The March 2025 per-staff pricing change drew "greedy" and "deceptively expensive". Booksy: a business owner of years says they were wrongly charged for "boost" clients; a client was charged a cancellation fee twice. Treatwell: monthly fee plus commission commonly reported around 35% on new clients.
2. **Support that disappears.** Treatwell removed phone and email support and left a chatbot. Setmore chat "left me hanging for hours". Booksy owner of seven years: four chargeback cases with no clear final answer. Fresha owner: three different account reps in a year.
3. **The platform owns the client relationship.** Marketplace ranking is opaque and fees change. Owners feel cornered: accept new fees or lose the calendar and notifications.
4. **No-shows and cancellation rules that misfire.** Booksy cancellation fee "doesn't work properly", clients with expired cards can still book. Deposits are the fix most owners praise, when they work.
5. **Messaging is weak or costs extra.** One comparison notes Booksy has limited WhatsApp integration. Fresha charges $0.04 to $0.11 per WhatsApp message after the first 100 and sells its AI message answering as a paid add-on.
6. **Reports and flexibility.** Fresha inventory reports that do not reconcile; Setmore has limited per-service rules.

## The needle

Trust and predictability: **no commission, no surprise fees, the client stays yours, and a real person answers.** Every big competitor's loudest complaints are about money surprises and unreachable support. Lexi runs on the salon's own WhatsApp number, so the client relationship never leaves her phone, and the price is a flat monthly fee.

## Careful

- "A real person answers" is a promise Theo has to keep. It limits how many clients one person can serve well, so the five founding clients are the right size.
- "No limits" is not safe until the real cost per booking is measured (Make credits plus Claude cost).
- Salonz and Zolmi have too few reviews to judge. Treat them as early, not as proven weak.
