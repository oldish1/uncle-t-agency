# Research: what's winning in web design (17 Oct 2026)

Sources pulled with Firecrawl: Awwwards (Sites of the Day, 15-16 Oct 2026), Figma's 2026 trends guide, Vistaprint/99designs 2026 trends, Fresh Move Media and Studio Web Design (small-business 2026 trends).

## What award sites do (Awwwards, Figma)

- Bold, oversized typography carrying the whole hero. Type is the design.
- Motion with purpose: scroll-triggered reveals, smooth transitions, micro-interactions on buttons and cards.
- Confident colour: a restricted palette with one vivid accent, or a strong dark mode.
- Experimental navigation and 3D/immersive scenes (WebGL). Impressive, but heavy.
- Collage, maximalism and neo-brutalism for brands that want attitude.
- Real photography and visual storytelling over stock images.

## What small-business sites need (Fresh Move, Studio Web Design, Vistaprint)

- Conversion-focused layout: one clear action above the fold, repeated down the page.
- Speed is trust. Slow pages lose visitors and rankings.
- Accessibility (contrast, tap targets, readable type) is now a baseline.
- Content structured so AI search can quote it: clear headings, short answers, FAQ blocks, schema markup.
- Local relevance: suburb names, Google Business link, map, reviews, "near me" wording.
- Light, human touches trending in 2026: "Snug Simple" (calm, uncluttered), "Human Scribble" (hand-drawn marks), "Frosted Touch" (soft glass effects), "Cute-alism" (friendly rounded shapes).

## The honest read for Uncle T Agency

Awwwards winners are built for design juries, often with 3D and heavy motion that wrecks phone speed. A Bellville salon needs a site that wins customers on a mid-range Android on mobile data, not a jury. So the Web Builder borrows the craft (type, imagery, restrained motion, art direction) and refuses the weight. "Award-winning" here means: looks like nothing else in the niche, scores 90+ on Lighthouse mobile, books customers.
