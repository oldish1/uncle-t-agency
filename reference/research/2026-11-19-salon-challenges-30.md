# 30 observable salon challenges, and which ones owners pay to fix

Run 19 Nov 2026 for Theo. Research with Firecrawl, plus what we already collected ourselves (the auto-replies from 36 salons, the competitor research of 25 to 29 Oct, the Google review pull of 29 Oct).

**This is desk research, not first-hand salon experience.** Treat every row as a hypothesis until the 20 conversations (see `outputs/sales/universal-discovery-script.md`) confirm it. The Conversations tab in the Pipeline Sheet is where the proof goes.

## How to read it

- **Observable** means you can see or hear it in a salon, in a WhatsApp chat or in a phone, without asking.
- **Pay verdict:**
  - **Paying now** = owners already spend money on a fix, so a market exists.
  - **Would pay** = costs real money or hours, owner is the buyer, and nobody serves it well yet.
  - **Annoying** = real, but the owner won't pay much (or the fix is free).
- **Evidence:** A = owner voice or our own data from salons we contacted. B = independent research or news. C = vendor blog (they sell the fix, so numbers lean high). D = our inference.

## What owners already pay for (rand where we have it)

| Spend | What we found | Source note |
|---|---|---|
| Booking software | Booklink R79 a month flat. Salonz R199, R499, R999 tiers. Fresha about R135 solo plus about R90 per team member (verify at fresha.com/pricing before quoting). Yoco appointments on its Plus plan at R249 a month | Our 29 Oct research plus a SA card-machine guide (5 Nov 2026) |
| Card machine | Yoco Khumo 2 and iKhokha iK Flyer Lite R699, Yoco Counter R3,499. Fees 2.30% plus VAT (about 2.645% in total) | SA card-machine guide, an affiliate site, so check each provider |
| Deposits | Free payment links on Yoco and iKhokha | Same guide |
| Commission | 40 to 50% of a stylist's takings is the ask in SA owner groups | A: owner-group post |
| Marketing | Ads, freelancers and photographers. We have no SA rand figures | Survey (US) |
| Training | Courses, SETA learnerships | Services SETA |

Pay verdict across the list: owners already spend real money on payments, booking tools, marketing and staff. Nobody we found sells a fix for **the WhatsApp conversation itself** (problems 1, 2, 3 and 8). That gap is the opening, but it also means we have to prove they'll pay, because no one has trained them to.

## What I think they would actually pay for (ranked)

1. **Missed and slow WhatsApp enquiries (#1, #3, #8).** Each unanswered message is a booking at risk. Owners already spend time on it (auto-replies, night replies).
2. **No-shows and unfilled slots (#4, #5).** Easy to put a rand figure on. At R350 a booking, two no-shows a month is about R700, which is more than R499.
3. **Time back (#7, #30).** Owners burn out. Hours saved are valuable, but harder to price.
4. **Rebooking and slow-season nudges (#10, #28, #27).** Reminders already exist in Lexi. Selling them is easy once the owner sees a pattern.
5. **Marketing return (#24, #25).** Already paying, so the budget exists. A different product from Lexi.
6. **Commission and diary tracking (#13, #14, #20).** Real, but Yoco and others already include it.

## Sources, and how much to trust them

- Services SETA, *Rapid Appraisal of the South African hairdressing industry* (2023): independent, SA, about training and compliance, not about bookings.
- Elite Beauty Society survey of 807 beauty professionals (July 2026): a US insurer and school, direct survey, but US.
- Cochrane systematic review on SMS reminders (Gurol-Urganci et al., 2013, via Etisia): peer-reviewed. Etisia sells reminders.
- Mashjoy, a SA card-machine guide (5 Nov 2026): SA prices, affiliate links.
- Boulevard, Salonist and Booksy blogs: vendors, so treat their percentages as directional.
- South African salon-owner Facebook groups: owner voices, but I could only read the search snippets, not the full threads.
- Our own: the auto-reply screenshots from 36 salons, the competitor research (`reference/research/2026-10-29-competitor-complaints.md`) and the Google review pull.

## Not found

- Load shedding and generators: no 2026 salon-specific evidence found. Ask owners.
- SA rand figures for social media freelancers and ad spend: none found. Ask owners what they pay.
- A clean source for "the owner pays more for X in Cape Town" beyond Services SETA and the owner-group posts. The 20 chats fix this.
