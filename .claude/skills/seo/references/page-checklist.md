# Service page checklist (for Google and for AI answers)

A service page is one page per treatment or job: "Nanoplastia in Bellville". AI tools and Google quote pages that answer one question clearly.

1. Title: service + place + business name ("Nanoplastia in Bellville, Cape Town | CHALES Hair Boutique").
2. Description: the price and the place in one sentence.
3. One H1 that matches the title.
4. First 60 words: direct answer. What it is, where, for whom.
5. Facts table: price (by hair length or size), how long it takes, how long it lasts, what's different.
6. Real price in rand, with an "updated <month year>" line. Refresh monthly.
7. 4 to 6 FAQs written like people ask them, with FAQPage schema.
8. Business schema: name, address, phone, hours, coordinates, the service and its offers.
9. Real photos with descriptive file names and alt text (not IMG-2026...), under 150 KB each.
10. One clear button: WhatsApp the booking assistant, plus phone for questions.
11. Same name, address, phone and hours as the Google listing.
12. Google Maps embed of the real listing, once the listing is verified.
13. Linked from the home page and listed in sitemap.xml.
14. No claims you can't back (stars without reviews, "best in Cape Town").
