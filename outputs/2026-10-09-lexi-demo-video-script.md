# Lexi demo video: shot list

> One screen recording on your phone, 60 to 90 seconds, sent to prospects on WhatsApp. Records the demo number (076 492 4196), not Chales' real number, so no client data shows.

## Before you hit record (5 minutes)

1. Phone on Do Not Disturb, battery above 50%, notifications off so nothing pops over the recording.
2. Use a second phone or WhatsApp Web to be the "customer", so the chat shows Lexi's replies landing on the demo number. If you only have one phone, message the demo number from your personal WhatsApp and record that chat.
3. Start the phone's built-in screen recorder (swipe down, Screen Record). Do one throwaway run first so you know the timing.
4. Check the demo number's calendar is empty for the day you'll book, so the day and time lists look full and real.

## The shots, in order

| Time | What's on screen | What you say (voiceover or caption) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Empty chat with the demo number. You type "Hi". | "A client messages your salon at 9pm." |
| 0:05 | Lexi's welcome and the service list land straight away. | "Reply is instant. No waiting for you to finish a client." |
| 0:12 | Tap a service (Wash and Blowdry). | "She lists your services, they just tap." |
| 0:18 | Day list appears. Tap Saturday. | "Only days you're actually open." |
| 0:25 | Time list. Tap 10:00. | "Only times that are really free. She checks your calendar, so no double bookings." |
| 0:32 | She asks for the name. Type it. | "Takes the name for the booking." |
| 0:40 | Confirmation with salon photo, day, time, and the Get Directions button. | "Confirmed, with the address and directions." |
| 0:50 | Cut to the calendar showing the new booking. | "And it's already in your calendar." |
| 0:58 | Back in chat, type "cancel". Show the cancel confirm and the "different date instead?" offer. | "Cancel or move it any time, no phone call to you." |
| 1:10 | End card text: "R1,499 once-off. R499/month. Live in 4 days." + your WhatsApp number. | "Want this on your salon's WhatsApp? Message me." |

## Optional extras if there's time

- A voice note shot: send a voice note saying "Saterdag, half tien" and show Lexi understanding it. This is a strong one for Afrikaans-speaking owners.
- Add Afrikaans captions as a second cut for Bellville and Parow.

## What not to show

- Chales' real number or real client names.
- The word "AI" or "chatbot" in captions. Lead with "receptionist who never sleeps and never double-books."

## Sending it

Send the video to a prospect with one line: "Hi [name], this is what I mentioned. It's a receptionist for your WhatsApp, 90 seconds. Want me to show you on your own number?" Start with the five warm leads: Zanzibar Hair and Beauty, Sistergirl, Reeva Hair, JEM Hair and Beauty Studio, Jason at No Love Lost.
