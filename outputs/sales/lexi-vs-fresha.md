# Lexi vs Fresha

Prices checked on fresha.com/pricing on 26 Oct 2026. Fresha bills in US dollars, shown here at about R18 to the dollar. Check the rate before quoting.

## The short version

Fresha is a booking system with an AI add-on. Lexi is a personal assistant that happens to take bookings. For a solo salon owner with her hands in someone's hair, the difference is who answers the WhatsApp.

## What each one costs a solo salon

| | Fresha | Lexi |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Free | R1,499 once-off |
| Monthly software | $19.95 (about R360) | R499 |
| Answers client messages for you | Extra: AI Concierge, $29.95 (about R540) a month | Included |
| Commission on new clients from their marketplace | 20% of the first booking, minimum $6 (about R110) | None |
| Card payments | 2.79% + $0.20 each | Not handled by Lexi |
| WhatsApp reminders | 100 free a month, then $0.04 to $0.11 each | Included |
| Year one, with message answering | about R10,800 | about R7,500 |

Be straight about this: Fresha on its own, with no AI, is cheaper than Lexi (about R360 against R499 a month). Lexi wins when the owner wants her WhatsApp answered.

## Where Lexi is better for a solo salon

1. **Clients stay in WhatsApp.** No app to download, no account to make. They message like they always do.
2. **The reply sounds like the salon.** "Your salon's personal assistant", not a software brand.
3. **No commission.** The clients you bring in are yours.
4. **Voice notes work.** A client can send a voice note and still get booked.
5. **Runs on the owner's own calendar.** Nothing new to learn.
6. **Local.** Rands, Cape Town, set up by someone who answers the phone.

## Where Fresha is better, today

- Point of sale, stock, gift cards, payroll and a full business app.
- Card payments and deposits built in.
- Multiple stylists on one calendar. Lexi does not do this yet (see `plans/2026-10-26-multi-stylist.md`).

## What to say, in one line

"Fresha is a calendar. Lexi answers your WhatsApp while you work. Your first month is free."

## Rules for using this

- Never run Fresha down. Say what they do and what we do differently.
- Never promise a feature that isn't built. Multi-stylist, deposits and payments are not built.
