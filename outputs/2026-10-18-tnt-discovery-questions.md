# T & T Engineering: find the leaks (question lists)

How to run it
- Talk to the receptionist first, at her desk, for 30 to 45 minutes. Ask her to show you real things, not describe them.
- Ask "can you show me the last one?" for every task. Real examples beat opinions.
- Write down the time each task takes and how often it happens. We turn that into hours per week.
- Do not promise fixes in the meeting. Say "I'm finding where the time goes first."
- Ask permission before taking photos or copies of documents. Customer details stay private.

## For the receptionist

Her day
1. Walk me through yesterday, hour by hour. What did you do first, and what took the most time?
2. Which tasks do you do every single day? Which pile up?
3. What do you do when the phone rings while you are busy with something else?
4. What do you never get to, because there is no time?

Orders
5. How does an order arrive (phone, email, WhatsApp, walk-in, drawings)? Roughly how many of each per week?
6. Show me the last order. What happens to it from first message to finished job?
7. Where do you write the order down? How many places does the same detail get typed?
8. How does the workshop find out what to make? How do you know when it is done?
9. How often does an order go wrong or get missed? What was the last one?
10. How do customers ask "where is my order?" and how long does it take you to answer?

Quotes
11. How many quote requests come in per week? How many turn into jobs?
12. Show me the last quote. How long did it take from request to sending it?
13. Who works out the price? Do you wait for someone? How long?
14. How do you follow up on quotes that get no answer?

Invoices and money
15. Show me how you write an invoice. Which program or template? How long does one take?
16. Where does the invoice information come from (the order, the quote, a notebook)?
17. How many invoices per week? How many get queried or sent wrong?
18. How do you know who has not paid? Who chases them, and how?
19. Do you also do statements, deposits, payments or bookkeeping tasks?

Email and messages
20. How many emails a day? What are most of them about?
21. Which email do you write again and again, almost the same each time?
22. Do customers send drawings or files? Where do they end up?

Systems
23. What programs do you use every day (accounting, Excel, email, WhatsApp)? Which do you dislike?
24. If you could remove one task from your day tomorrow, which one?

Wish list
25. What do customers complain about most?
26. If you had a helper that never got tired, what would you hand over first?

## For the owner (Errol and his son)

Business and goals
1. What does a good year look like? What would you like to grow?
2. Which customers and jobs make you the most money? Which cause the most trouble?
3. Where do new customers come from today?

Where money leaks
4. Where do you think time or money is lost? What does it cost you in a normal week?
5. How many quotes go out, and how many become jobs? What happens to the rest?
6. Do jobs get redone, lost, delayed or invoiced late? How often?
7. How long between finishing a job and getting paid?

Quotes and pricing (needed for an after-hours assistant)
8. Who prices a job? What does the price depend on (material, thickness, cutting time, bends, quantity)?
9. Are there standard rules or a price list? Could a clerk price simple jobs from it?
10. Which jobs must always be checked by Errol or you? Which could be quoted by the rule?

Production
11. How does the workshop get its list of jobs? Who decides the order they are made in?
12. What is the biggest bottleneck: the laser, the press brake, material, drawings, or staff?
13. How busy are the new laser and press brake? Is there spare capacity?

After hours and customers
14. What do customers do when they call or message after 16:30 or on a weekend? Do you lose work?
15. Would you like requests answered at any hour? What should it say or not say?

Tools and access
16. Which accounting and email systems do you use? Who owns the logins?
17. Are you happy for us to look at sample documents (quote, order, invoice) with customer details hidden?

Decisions
18. What would make this worth doing? How many hours saved or rand recovered would you call a win?
19. Who decides, and who must be comfortable with it (including the receptionist)?
20. Is there anything you do not want automated?

## Ask for these documents
A recent quote, order and invoice (names hidden), the price list if there is one, a week of the shared inbox (subjects only), and a screenshot of the program used for invoices.

## After the meetings (Claude does this with Theo)
Make one table: task, who does it, minutes each time, times per week, hours per week, what goes wrong. The biggest number is the first leak to fix.
