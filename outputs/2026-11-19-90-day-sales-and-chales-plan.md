## The cinematic reels (Higgsfield)

**How it works:**
1. Chanté films raw clips on her phone while she works: the before, hands working, the product, the hair flip, the client's reaction at the mirror. Vertical, good light, 5 to 15 seconds each. No editing, no talking needed.
2. She sends them to you on WhatsApp. You drop them into a session here and say "make a Chales reel".
3. I upload them to Higgsfield and cut them like a movie trailer: slow-motion hair moments, cinematic colour grade, dramatic cuts on the beat, title cards ("BEFORE" / "4 HOURS LATER"), a closing card with the price and Lexi's number.
4. You check it, post it to TikTok, Instagram Reels and Google.

**Important rule:** the transformation itself must be real footage of Chanté's real work. AI can make it look like a movie (grading, slow-mo, cuts, titles, cinematic intro shots of the salon), but it never fakes a result. A fake before/after would get her called out in the comments and it's false advertising. Real work, movie-quality edit.
