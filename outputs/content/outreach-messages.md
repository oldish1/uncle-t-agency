# Outreach messages (copy, edit to sound like you, send)

Rule: never send the same message to a hundred people at once from the business number. Send one by one, with the owner's name and the salon name filled in.

## First message (WhatsApp or Instagram DM)
Hi [Name], it's Theo from Bellville. I saw [Salon name] and love your work. Quick question: how many calls and messages do you miss when you're busy with a client?

I built a WhatsApp receptionist called Lexi that answers customers, takes the bookings and sends reminders, even at 9pm. She's already running at a salon in Bellville. You can try her on your own phone: 076 492 4196. Just say hi.

No pressure. If it's useful I can set it up for you in about four days.

## Follow-up, 2 days later
Hi [Name], just checking you saw my message about Lexi. Did you get a chance to try her on 076 492 4196? Happy to show you live in 5 minutes, or I can come past the salon.

## Follow-up, 5 days later (last one)
Hi [Name], last note from me. If missed bookings aren't a problem right now, no worries at all. If they ever are, my number is here. All the best with the busy season.

## When they say "how much?"
R1,499 once-off setup, then R799 a month for Lexi. If you want the website as well, it's R999 a month for both. No contract. We're live in about four days.

Fair enough. Quick question though, when you're with a client and someone messages, what happens to that booking?
