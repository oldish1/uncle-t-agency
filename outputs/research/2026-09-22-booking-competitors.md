# Salon booking competitors: what they offer, what people hate, where Lexi wins

Research date: 22 September 2026. Built from web searches of pricing guides, review sites (Capterra, Trustpilot, G2, Hellopeter, app stores) and the competitors' own pages. Most competitor websites were blocked from this machine, so a lot of this comes from search-result summaries and third-party pricing guides, not the vendors' live pricing pages. Anything I couldn't confirm is marked **(unverified)**. Dollar prices are converted at roughly R18 to the dollar, so treat those Rand figures as ballpark.

---

## 1. Bottom line

The big platforms (Fresha, Booksy, Timely, Vagaro, Phorest) all do far more than Lexi: payments, stock, staff commission, marketing, reports. They all make the salon (and usually the client) leave WhatsApp and use their app or a web link. The complaints about them are the same every time: surprise fees (Fresha's 20% and Booksy's 30% cut on "new" clients who were really the salon's own), prices that climb per staff member, support you can't reach, and clients who won't download another app. Meanwhile the cheap South African tools are getting good. Booklink does WhatsApp reminders and Yoco deposits for R79 a month, and GetMeBooked does deposits and SMS reminders for R299. So Lexi can't win on features or on price per month. It wins if it stays a real conversation inside the salon's own WhatsApp number, in English and Afrikaans, answers in seconds, never takes a cut, and comes with a local person who sets it up and picks up the phone. The two gaps that matter most right now are the cancel/reschedule flow and deposits via a Yoco or PayFast link, because no-shows are the pain every competitor sells against.

## 3. Competitor profiles

### Fresha (global, big in SA)

- **Price:** Was "free" until 2025. From 1 August 2025 it charges **R90 per bookable team member per month** in South Africa (reported by Capterra SA and Software Advice, not checked on Fresha's own page). On top of that: 20 free messages a month, then paid SMS/WhatsApp credits; a **20% one-off fee** (US minimum $6) on a new client's first visit if they came through the Fresha marketplace; card processing fees (SA rate unverified).
- **Suits:** Salons with 2 to 10 staff who want everything in one place and don't mind their clients using the Fresha app.
- **Strengths:** Huge feature list for the price (POS, stock, commission, memberships, gift cards). Deposits and card on file for no-show fees. Bookings from Instagram, Facebook and Google.
- **Top complaints:**
  1. The 20% new-client fee gets charged on clients who found the salon through its own Instagram, Google or word of mouth, because the client tapped "new" in the app. ([Sort The Clicks](https://sorttheclicks.com/fresha-reviews-reddit/), [TimeTailor summary of Reddit/Trustpilot](https://www.timetailor.com/timetailor-alternatives/fresha-reddit-reviews), [Capterra](https://capterra.com/p/142138/Shedul-com/reviews/))
  2. "They said they wouldn't charge but are now charging." SA users called the 2025 switch "unfair and unsolicited." ([Capterra SA](https://www.capterra.co.za/software/142138/shedul-com), [Software Advice reviews](https://www.softwareadvice.com/retail/shedul-profile/reviews/))
  3. No phone support, slow copy-paste email replies, payouts held with no one to call (mostly UK threads). ([TimeTailor](https://www.timetailor.com/timetailor-alternatives/fresha-reddit-reviews), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/fresha.com))
  - Also: deposits aren't forced at booking, so a receptionist has to chase who's paid. ([Capterra reviews](https://capterra.com/p/142138/Shedul-com/reviews/))

### Booksy (global, operates in SA)

- **Price:** US public price $29.99/month for one user plus $20 per extra user (about R540 and R360). SA price **unverified**. **Boost** marketing takes **30%** of a new client's first visit (US min $10, max $100). Card processing about 2.5 to 2.7% in the US.
- **Suits:** Barbers and solo stylists who want a marketplace listing and are happy for clients to use the Booksy app.
- **Strengths:** Strong no-show protection (deposits or cancellation fees on a stored card). Book buttons on Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps. Online gift cards.
- **Top complaints:**
  1. Boost charges on clients who were already the salon's, or who found them on Google first. Disputes go nowhere because "Boost is fully automated." ([Trustpilot UK](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/booksy.com), [Setora on Boost](https://www.setora.co.uk/blog/booksy-boost-commission-explained))
  2. Clients have to download the app to book. ([Capterra via search](https://www.capterra.com/compare/32216-142741/ProSolutions-Software-vs-Booksy))
  3. Pricier than rivals and hard to reach support. A Hellopeter review is titled "Most useless company in South Africa" (I couldn't open it to read the detail). ([Capterra SA](https://www.capterra.co.za/reviews/142741/booksy), [Hellopeter](https://www.hellopeter.com/booksy/reviews/most-useless-company-in-south-africa-3cc9339e442966d63d6c2e8c963afe1164268ea9-4283866))

### Setmore (global, general scheduling)

- **Price:** Free for up to 4 users and 200 appointments a month (email reminders only). Paid plans from $5 to $9 per user per month (about R90 to R160), which adds SMS reminders.
- **Suits:** A solo stylist who wants a free booking page and can live with email reminders.
- **Strengths:** Genuinely usable free plan. Booking page links from Instagram, Facebook and Google. 24/7 support is well rated.
- **Top complaints:**
  1. Google Calendar sync is slow or glitchy. ([Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/setmore-review/), [Trafft](https://trafft.com/setmore-reviews/))
  2. SMS reminders and other basics locked behind paid tiers. ([Pabau pricing](https://pabau.com/blog/setmore-pricing/))
  3. Not built for salons: thin package handling, no per-service rules, weak Android app (3.8 stars). ([Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/setmore-review/))

### Timely (NZ-built, sold in SA)

- **Price:** Per bookable staff member. US: $26, $39 or $47 per staff per month (about R470 to R850 each). SA Rand pricing **unverified**.
- **Suits:** Established salons with 3+ staff who want polished software and will pay for it.
- **Strengths:** Clean, easy calendar. Good reminders and SMS campaigns (available in SA). Month to month, no contract.
- **Top complaints:**
  1. Bill grows with every hire, part-timers cost the same as full-timers. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/142756/Timely/))
  2. Booking bugs: available slots not showing, clients booked into blocked times. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/142756/Timely/))
  3. "Price increase with no improvement in service," slow or dismissive support. ([Software Advice](https://softwareadvice.com/salon/timely-profile/reviews), [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/customer-management-software/a/timely/))

### Vagaro (US)

- **Price:** $23.99/month with one calendar, plus $10 per extra staff calendar (about R430, plus R180 each). Add-ons: text marketing $20, forms $10, website $20. SA availability **unverified**, it's mainly US/UK/Canada/Australia.
- **Suits:** Not really an SA option.
- **Strengths:** Big feature set, marketplace, POS.
- **Complaints:** Add-on fees for things people assumed were included; expensive texting; dated calendar. ([Pabau](https://pabau.com/blog/vagaro-pricing/), [Koalendar](https://koalendar.com/blog/vagaro-pricing))

### Square Appointments

- **Not available in South Africa.** Square doesn't process cards here. ([Square Community](https://community.squareup.com/t5/Archived-Discussions-Read-Only/What-Features-are-are-availble-for-South-Africa-on-Square/m-p/155196), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_(financial_services))) Ignore it. For reference, US price is free for one location or $49/month for Plus.

### Phorest (Irish, premium)

- **Price:** Quote only. Third parties say from about $99/month (roughly R1,800), with some users quoting around $300. SA presence and pricing **unverified**.
- **Suits:** Bigger salons with a front desk.
- **Strengths:** Strong reporting, good marketing tools, some users praise the support.
- **Complaints:** Contracts that weren't explained at sale, charged after a free trial, too expensive for small salons. ([SelectHub](https://www.selecthub.com/p/spa-software/phorest/), [GetApp](https://www.getapp.com/customer-management-software/a/phorest-salon-software/))

### Zolmi (UK-built, has a dedicated SA site)

- **Price:** "Manage" plan free forever (unlimited staff, appointments, email/in-app reminders, booking page, Google Calendar sync). "Grow" paid plan adds SMS, marketing, gift cards, memberships, review booster. Third-party listings say $9 to $15/month (about R160 to R270). Grow's **Rand price unverified** (the SA pricing page was blocked).
- **Suits:** A price-sensitive salon that wants proper salon software for free or cheap.
- **Strengths:** Very generous free plan. Google review booster that users say works ("10+ new reviews in a month"). Bookings from Google Business, Instagram and Facebook.
- **Complaints (few, rated 4.8 on Capterra from 26 reviews):**
  1. Not enough free SMS and **no WhatsApp notifications**. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177810/Belliata/reviews/))
  2. Can't sell gift vouchers online without a workaround. ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177810/Belliata/reviews/))
  3. One reviewer: "expensive with lots of hidden costs" (others say it's fair). ([Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/177810/Belliata/reviews/))

### Booklink (SA, the real local threat)

- **Price:** Free for 30 bookings/month, one service, one team member. **Pro R79/month**: unlimited bookings, up to 5 team members, Google Calendar, **WhatsApp reminders**, deposits through Yoco, Paystack and PayFast. No commission.
- **Suits:** Exactly Lexi's customer if they're happy to send clients a link.
- **Strengths:** Built for SA payment gateways. WhatsApp reminders as a main feature. Very cheap. Says it's POPIA compliant.
- **Complaints:** None found. It's new and has almost no public reviews, so I can't say how reliable it is. ([Booklink](https://booklink.co.za/online-booking-system-south-africa/), [Booklink features](https://booklink.co.za/features/))

### GetMeBooked (SA)

- **Price:** R299/month flat, unlimited staff, deposits, SMS reminders, vouchers, CRM, reports. There's a per-booking transaction fee on that plan (amount unverified). Premium R499/month flat removes it.
- **Suits:** Solo or small salons, nail bars, lash studios.
- **Strengths:** PayFast and Paystack deposits, vouchers included, flat price.
- **Complaints:** None found (too new or too small for reviews). ([GetMeBooked](https://www.getmebooked.co.za/))

### Nextapt (SA, WhatsApp booking)

- **Price:** R1 per booking, no monthly fee, no setup, 10 free bookings to start. It looks like the **client** pays the R1 through an online payment step. ([Nextapt](https://nextapt.co.za/), [Nextapt pilot page](https://pilot.observable.co.za/))
- **How it works:** Salon prints a QR code, client scans, books through a WhatsApp flow, both get a WhatsApp confirmation, booking shows in a dashboard. Salon needs WhatsApp Business to get notifications.
- **Suits:** Very small operators who want zero monthly cost.
- **Strengths:** Almost free. WhatsApp-native. Two-minute setup.
- **Weak spots (my read, not from reviews):** Asking a client to pay R1 to book is friction. It seems to be a fixed flow, not a chat that understands "can I come Saturday after 2 for knotless braids?" No reviews found. Whether it runs on the salon's own number or a shared Nextapt number is **unverified**.

### WAFlowBot (WhatsApp flow builder)

- **Price:** Own subscription price **unverified**. You also need an UltraMsg WhatsApp key at **$39/month** (about R700). ([WAFlowBot](https://waflowbot.com/), [BizAI guide](https://www.bizai.co.za/blog/whatsapp-chatbot-south-africa-setup-cost-2026))
- **What it does:** Drag-and-drop WhatsApp flows, Google Calendar and iCal booking, contact tags, "smart AI." A salon-flavoured landing page sells it as "Turn WhatsApp Into Your 24/7 Booking Machine." ([funthingssa landing page](https://goal.funthingssa.co.za/))
- **Weak spot:** It's DIY. The salon owner has to build the flows. UltraMsg, as far as I know, connects through WhatsApp Web rather than Meta's official API, which carries a risk of the number being banned (my understanding, not verified for this product).

### BizAI (SA)

- **Price:** AI Inbox R299/month, Comms Platform R500/month (3 users, 1 number, extra user R99), business platform from R599/month. Plus Meta's per-message fees on outbound messages. 30-day free trial. ([BizAI pricing](https://www.bizai.co.za/pricing), [BizAI WhatsApp](https://www.bizai.co.za/whatsapp-automation))
- **What it does:** Shared team WhatsApp inbox, AI auto-replies, broadcasts, CRM. General SME tool, not salon booking specific. Calendar booking not confirmed.

### Lekker AI (SA)

- **Price:** R499/month basic (250 messages, FAQs and after-hours, website chat), R999/month adds a "continue on WhatsApp" button, up to R4,999. Setup included, VAT included. ([Lekker AI salons](https://www.lekkerai.co.za/ai-chatbot-salon-south-africa))
- **What it does:** A website chatbot that collects service, stylist, date and contact details. Confirmation happens in a dashboard, so it's a lead catcher, not a live calendar booker. At R499 it doesn't even live inside WhatsApp.

### Telecloud

- I couldn't find any WhatsApp booking product from Telecloud. Their public site is about cloud telecoms, websites and ERP. The R2,000 to R11,000 website pricing in `context/offer.md` still stands as Theo's own research; I couldn't re-verify it.

## 5. Where Lexi can beat them

1. **"Your clients stay yours. No cut, ever."** Fresha takes 20% and Booksy takes 30% of "new" clients, and owners keep getting charged on regulars. Lexi charges a flat R499 and never touches a booking. Say this out loud in every pitch.
2. **No app, no link, no login for the client.** Booksy users complain clients won't download the app. Lexi's clients just WhatsApp the number they already have saved. For older and Afrikaans-speaking clients who don't do apps, that's the whole point.
3. **It actually talks.** Every other tool, including Nextapt and WAFlowBot, is a form or a button flow. Lexi understands "can my daughter and I come Saturday for cornrows, after netball?" That's the demo moment.
4. **Afrikaans and English, automatically.** I found no competitor that markets Afrikaans. Cheap to do, and it speaks to Bellville, Parow, Goodwood and Mitchells Plain directly.
5. **A person in Cape Town who sets it up and answers the phone.** Fresha, Timely and Booksy all get hammered for unreachable support. Theo can be the opposite: "you WhatsApp me, I fix it today." Keep that promise small enough to keep.
6. **No price creep.** Fresha went from free to R90 per staff member and users felt tricked. Timely and Vagaro grow with every hire and add-on. Lexi at a flat R499 for the whole salon is easy to trust. Put "price locked for founding clients" in writing.
7. **Done for you in 4 days.** Setmore, Zolmi and Booklink are cheap because the owner does the setup. Owners who keep a paper diary won't. Lexi's setup fee buys them out of that.
8. **It doesn't break the owner's calendar.** Setmore's top complaint is Google Calendar sync. Lexi uses Google Calendar as the source of truth, so there's nothing to sync. Only true if the rebuild is reliable, so test double-booking hard.

Honest warning: points 3 and 7 only hold if Lexi is fast and reliable. A slow or confused bot is worse than a boring booking link, and Booklink's link costs R79.

## 6. Pricing positioning

Monthly cost for the booking tool only (not counting card fees, message credits or marketplace commissions unless noted). Dollar prices converted at about R18 to the dollar.

| Tool | 1-person salon | 3-person salon | Extra costs to watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lexi** | **R499** (+ R1,499 once-off) | **R499** (+ R1,499 once-off) | Meta message fees are on Theo, not the salon (see note below) |
| Booklink Pro | R79 (or free under 30 bookings) | R79 | Payment gateway fees on deposits |
| GetMeBooked | R299 (+ per-booking fee) or R499 Premium | R299 or R499 | Per-booking fee on the R299 plan (amount unverified) |
| Zolmi | Free (Manage), Grow roughly R160 to R270 (unverified) | Same | Paid SMS |
| Setmore | Free (email reminders) or about R90 to R160 | Free or about R270 to R490 | SMS on paid plans only |
| Fresha | R90 | R270 | 20% on marketplace new clients, message credits after 20 free, card fees |
| Nextapt | About R0 (client pays R1 per booking) | Same | Client friction |
| Vagaro | About R430 | About R790 | Text marketing, website and forms add-ons |
| Booksy | About R540 (US price) | About R1,260 (US price) | 30% Boost fee, card fees |
| Timely | About R470 to R850 | About R1,400 to R2,540 | SMS over allowance |
| Phorest | About R1,800+ (quote only) | Quote | Contracts |
| BizAI | R299 to R599 (inbox, not a booking system) | R500+ | Meta message fees |
| Lekker AI | R499 (website chat only) to R999 (WhatsApp button) | Same | Message caps |

**What this means:**

- **For a 1-person salon, Lexi is one of the most expensive options.** A solo braider can get Booklink with WhatsApp reminders and deposits for R79, or Fresha for R90. The sale has to be "you stop answering WhatsApps yourself," not "this is cheaper." First-year cost is R7,487 (R1,499 + 12 × R499) against about R948 for Booklink Pro.
- **For a 3-person salon, Lexi is in the middle.** Cheaper than Booksy, Timely or Phorest, dearer than Fresha (R270 before fees) and the SA link tools. The flat price is a real advantage here: a 4th stylist costs nothing extra.
- **Fresha's hidden costs close the gap.** One marketplace client on a R600 braiding job costs the salon R120 in Fresha's fee. Two or three of those a month and Fresha costs more than Lexi.
- **Meta's message fees affect Theo's margin.** WhatsApp Business charges per template message sent outside a chat that the client started (reminders, review requests, rebooking nudges). Estimates for SA utility messages are around R0.15 to R0.40 each, marketing messages more. Several sources say that **from 1 October 2026**, utility templates sent inside the 24-hour chat window will also be charged (they've been free since July 2025). I haven't confirmed that on Meta's own page, so check it before October. At 200 reminders a month that's roughly R30 to R80 per salon, fine at R499, but it adds up if Theo adds broadcasts and nudges without charging for them. ([ChatMaxima SA](https://chatmaxima.com/whatsapp-api-pricing/south-africa/), [Meta pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing))

**Pricing ideas to consider (Theo's call):**
- A cheaper "solo" tier (say R299) for one-chair braiders and nail techs, since that's where Booklink and Fresha undercut hardest.
- Keep R499 flat for the whole salon and say "no per-staff fees, no commission" loudly. It's the clearest difference from Fresha and Timely.
- Offer deposits as the reason to pay more. If Lexi collects a R100 deposit on every braids booking and cuts two no-shows a month, it pays for itself.
