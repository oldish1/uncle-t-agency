# How to load this into NotebookLM

1. Go to notebooklm.google.com and make a new notebook called "Uncle T Sales School".
2. Upload `02-uncle-t-sales-playbook.md` first (Add source > Upload, or copy and paste it as text). This is the one that ties everything to Lexi and your salons.
3. Open `01-youtube-links-for-notebooklm.txt` and add each YouTube link (Add source > YouTube). If you're short on time, add Module 2 and Module 3 first.
4. Click **Audio Overview > Customise** and paste one of the prompts below. It makes a podcast you can listen to in the car.

## Audio Overview prompts to paste

**Episode 1, the foundation:**
"Teach Theo, a Cape Town founder who sells WhatsApp booking assistants to hair salons, how to make salon owners comfortable and get them to talk about their pain. Focus on Chris Voss's mirroring, labelling and accusation audits, with real salon examples."

**Episode 2, the questions:**
"Walk through SPIN Selling, the Sandler pain funnel and Jeremy Miner's NEPQ questions. Give word-for-word questions Theo can ask a busy salon owner who misses WhatsApp messages and gets no-shows."

**Episode 3, closing and price:**
"Explain Alex Hormozi's CLOSER framework and how to handle 'it's too expensive' and 'I need to think about it' for a R499 a month product. Role-play two short examples."

**Episode 4, nerves and walk-ins:**
"Coach a builder who hates selling on how to walk into a salon cold, handle rejection, and treat every visit as research. Use Jeb Blount and the cold-calling videos."
