# Outreach for higher-ticket local businesses

Same rule as the salon messages: one real question, no offer, no "AI". These owners are busier and more guarded, so be shorter, name why you picked them, and make the question about their business, not about you.

Targets (Cape Town): med spas and aesthetics clinics, dentists, physios and chiropractors, vets, dog groomers, driving schools, car detailers, photographers, installers and tradespeople who quote jobs, private tutors and music schools.

Where to find them: Google Maps (search the type plus the suburb), their Instagram or Facebook page, the lead scraper in scripts/lead_scraper.py once Apify resets. Look for ones who list a WhatsApp number or "DM to book".

## 1. Cold message, English
Hi, Theo here, Cape Town. I help small businesses with WhatsApp. I'm talking to a few [practice owners / groomers / driving school owners] about what makes the admin side hard. Can I ask you one question? When a client doesn't pitch or cancels late, what does that cost you?

## 2. Cold message, Afrikaans
Hi, dis Theo in Kaapstad. Ek praat met 'n paar [praktyk-eienaars / eienaars van hondekappers] oor wat die admin kant moeilik maak. Mag ek een vraag vra? As 'n kliënt nie opdaag nie of laat kanselleer, wat kos dit jou?

## 3. Cold message, when they list a WhatsApp number or DM-to-book
Hi, I saw you take bookings on WhatsApp. How do you cope when messages come in after hours or while you are with a client? I'm trying to understand how businesses like yours actually manage it.

## 4. By phone or in person (practice managers and receptionists)
"Hi, my name is Theo. I'm not selling anything. I'm trying to learn how practices like yours handle bookings and no-shows. Could I ask the owner or practice manager two questions? I'll be quick."

## 5. Second question once they reply (pick one)
- Roughly how many appointments a month go empty because of a no-show or late cancel?
- What is one empty slot worth to you in rand?
- Who answers WhatsApp when you are busy, and what happens at night?
- What do you use for bookings today, and what do you pay for it?

## Do not
- Do not quote prices or say "R499". For these owners the number must come from them, not us.
- Do not criticise the tool they use now. Ask what they like and dislike about it.
- Do not say you are a student or a survey company.
- Do not push a call. If they reply with a number, thank them and ask the next question in the chat.

## Weekly plan
10 messages to higher-ticket owners across at least three business types, spread over Bellville, Parow, Goodwood, Durbanville, Tygervalley and the southern suburbs. Target 3 replies. Score every conversation on the sheet within an hour of finishing it.
