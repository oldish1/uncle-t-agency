# Meeting: how Lexi competes with Fresha and the rest

For: Theo. Prep reading: `outputs/research/2026-09-22-booking-competitors.md` (the full research, with sources).
Time needed: 30 minutes. Four decisions, in order.

## What the research found, in five lines

1. Lexi can't beat them on price or feature count. Booklink (South African) does WhatsApp reminders and Yoco deposits for R79 a month. Fresha is R90 per stylist.
2. Everyone hates the same things about the big platforms: Fresha takes 20% and Booksy 30% of "new" clients, even regulars; prices go up with every stylist; support never picks up; clients get forced into an app.
3. Nobody else lets a client simply chat in the salon's own WhatsApp, in Afrikaans or English, and get booked. Nextapt and WAFlowBot use tap-the-button menus, not a real conversation.
4. Nobody else gives salons a local person who sets it all up and answers the phone.
5. Most competitor prices came from review and pricing sites, not the vendors' own pages (those were blocked). Check any price on your phone before quoting it to a salon.

## Decision 1: what goes into Lexi 2.0 before the first paying client

Proposed "must-have" list. Everything else waits.

| # | Feature | Why it's on the list |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Replies in under 20 seconds | Already the blocker. Nothing else matters until this works. |
| 2 | Cancel and reschedule by chat | Every competitor has it. Already planned in `context/lexi.md`. |
| 3 | Day-before reminder with Confirm / Reschedule / Cancel buttons | The cheapest no-show fix there is. The buttons feed into #2. |
| 4 | Answers in whichever language the client writes (English or Afrikaans) | One line in Lexi's instructions. No competitor offers it. |
| 5 | "Let me get Chante for you" handoff | When Lexi isn't sure, she tells the client and pings the owner, instead of guessing. |

**Deposits** (a Yoco or PayFast payment link for braids and long bookings) come straight after these five. They're the biggest no-show weapon, but they're more work. Your call whether they go before or after the first client.

**Decide:** agree the list, or move items in or out.

## Decision 2: the "Next" list, after the first paying client

In rough order: morning WhatsApp summary to the owner ("today: 6 bookings, 1 unconfirmed"), Google review request after the visit, rebooking nudge ("it's been 6 weeks since your braids"), filling cancelled slots, several stylists each with their own diary, a WhatsApp QR code for the shop window and Instagram bio, and a list of repeat no-shows.

**Deliberately ignoring:** card machines and point-of-sale, stock, staff commission, a marketplace app, "Book with Google" buttons, memberships. Salons that need those use Fresha, and later Lexi can plug into Fresha instead of fighting it.

**Decide:** agree the order.

## Decision 3: pricing

Where R499 sits today:

| | 1-person salon, per month | 3-person salon, per month |
|---|---|---|
| Lexi | R499 | R499 |
| Booklink Pro | R79 | R79 |
| Fresha | R90 + 20% on marketplace clients | R270 + 20% on marketplace clients |
| GetMeBooked | R299 or R499 | R299 or R499 |
| Booksy (US price) | about R540 + 30% Boost fee | about R1,260 |
| Timely (US price) | about R470 to R850 | about R1,400 to R2,540 |

For a solo braider, Lexi is one of the dearest options. For a 3-person salon it's middle of the pack, and adding a 4th stylist costs nothing.

Options:
- **A.** Keep R499 flat and sell hard on "no commission, no per-stylist fees, no app, a local person."
- **B.** Add a solo tier at R299 for one-chair braiders and nail techs, keep R499 for salons with staff.
- **C.** Keep R499 but include deposits, so Lexi pays for itself after two saved no-shows.

**Decide:** A, B or C. Also whether founding clients get their price locked in writing (the research suggests yes, because of how badly Fresha's price change landed).

## Decision 4: WhatsApp message costs

Meta charges per reminder or nudge message. Several sources say that from 1 October 2026 Meta will start charging for reminders sent inside the 24-hour window, which are free now. That's unconfirmed. Rough cost: R30 to R80 a month per salon at 200 reminders, which is fine at R499. It gets expensive if broadcasts and nudges are added for free.

**Decide:** reminders stay included. Do broadcasts and rebooking nudges cost extra when they arrive? Action: check Meta's pricing page before 1 October.
