# Lexi walk-in pitch script

> For cold, in-person visits to any local salon with no website and no booking system, not just the five warm leads. Angle: their time and their missed money, not the technology. Adapt the bracketed bits on the fly, don't read it word for word.

---

## Before you walk in

Look for the signs first: paper diary on the counter, owner answering her own phone mid-cut, no booking link anywhere on her Instagram or Facebook. Those are your opening line, not "hi, I sell AI."

---

## The opening (30 seconds, standing at the counter)

"Hi, I'm Theo, I'm local, I build systems for salons around here so owners stop losing bookings. Got two minutes? I promise it's not a sales pitch, I just want to show you something on my phone."

If she's mid-client: "No rush, I'll pop back, when's good?" Leave a card, come back. Never pitch over a client's head.

---

## The pain hook

Ask, don't tell:

"Quick one, how many messages do you think you miss a week when you're with a client and can't check your phone?"

Let her answer. Whatever she says, follow with:

"And when someone messages you at 9pm asking for a Saturday slot, do you usually catch it before they've already booked somewhere else?"

Most owners will laugh or wince here, that's the moment. Don't explain the product yet. Sit in the pain for a second.

"That's the exact thing I fix. Every missed message is a missed booking, and every missed booking is money that walked to whoever answered first."

---

## The reveal, in one sentence

"I build a receptionist that lives inside your own WhatsApp, she never sleeps, never double-books, and she's already running for a salon just like yours in Bellville. Can I show you?"

Pull out your phone, message the demo number live in front of her. Let her watch it reply in real time, don't describe it, show it.

---

## What to actually demo

1. Send "Hi" to the demo number. Show the greeting and service list land instantly.
2. Tap a service, tap a day, tap a time. Point out: "See how fast that is? No typing, no back and forth."
3. Show the confirmation message with the photo and the directions button.
4. If she asks about cancellations: show a real cancel, and the "want a different date instead?" save.

Keep it under two minutes. She's a business owner, not a phone. Let the speed sell it, not your explanation of it.

---

## What it is, in her language

Never say "AI," "chatbot," "automation," or "technology" unless she says it first. Say:

"It's a receptionist who never sleeps and never double-books. She answers WhatsApp for you, day or night, books straight into your calendar, and reminds clients the day before so you get fewer no-shows."

If she asks "so it's a chatbot?": "Sort of, but she talks like a real person, not like Siri. Your clients won't even think twice."

---

## Price, only once she's asked or you've shown the demo

"R1,499 once-off to set it up, R499 a month after that, hosting and everything included. No contract, cancel anytime. We're live in about four days."

Anchor it if she flinches: "A missed booking probably costs you more than that in a week. This pays for itself the first weekend it catches one."

---

## Common pushback, and the honest answer

**"I already reply fast enough myself."**
"That's brilliant, most owners don't. This isn't about replacing you, it's about covering the hours you're physically with a client and can't."

**"What if it gets something wrong?"**
"It checks your real calendar before it books anything, so it physically can't double-book you. And if a client wants something it can't handle, you still see everything."

**"Is it hard to set up?"**
"Nothing on your end except giving me access to your WhatsApp Business number, I do the rest. Four days, then it's live."

**"I need to think about it."**
Never push. "Totally fair. Here's my number, and here's the demo number too, message it yourself tonight and see how it feels. I'll check in Thursday."

---

## The close

Don't ask "do you want to sign up." Ask for the next small step:

"Can I get your WhatsApp Business number so I can build it and show you the real thing running on your own account before you pay anything?"

Or, if she's not ready: "No pressure at all, save my number, and try messaging the demo yourself, 076 492 4196. If it feels right, message me."

---

## After the visit

Log it the same day: who, where, what she said, whether she's warm, lukewarm, or a no. A "no" today is a maybe in three months, don't burn the door.
