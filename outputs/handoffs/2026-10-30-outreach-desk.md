## Follow-ups (from the research)
- No reply: one follow-up on day 3 (Monday 2 Nov for today's sends) and one around day 10, then stop.
- Each under 50 words, one new fact. Example: *Hi [name], Theo again. In case the video got buried: Lexi answers your WhatsApp bookings while you're with a client. Flat R499 a month, no commission on any client, first month free. Worth a quick look? If not, no problem at all.*
- Auto-replies (away messages) are not replies. Only a human answer counts.
- A "no thanks" is permanent: never message that salon again.

## When someone replies

Paste what they said into the outreach session. Claude drafts the answer. Ask each owner one question: *How many WhatsApp messages do you miss on a busy day, and have you used a booking app that annoyed you?* Write their exact words down.
