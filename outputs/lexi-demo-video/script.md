# Lexi demo video: script and shot list

**Length:** 90 seconds. **Format:** vertical phone video (9:16), so it plays full-screen on WhatsApp.
**Record when:** Lexi replies in under 20 seconds. Don't record the slow version.
**Record on:** CHALES Hair Boutique's real number. Real bookings, real diary, nothing mocked up.

## What you need before recording

- Two phones. Phone A is "the client" (your own or Chante's personal number). Phone B shows Chante's Google Calendar.
- A phone screen recorder: Android has one built in (swipe down, Screen record). iPhone: Control Centre, Screen Recording.
- A stopwatch on screen, or add a timer in editing. The speed is the whole point, so show it.
- CapCut (free) to join the clips and add captions.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no other messages pop up during recording.

## Recording tips

- Do a few practice runs first, then record the fastest clean one. Use a real booking time you then delete from the calendar.
- Use a first name like "Lisa" for the client, never a real client's name or number.
- Record your voice separately in a quiet room and lay it over the screen clips in CapCut. It sounds far better than talking while tapping.
- Export at 1080 x 1920. WhatsApp compresses video, so keep the captions big.

## How it's used

The lead finder's first message now ends with a yes/no question: "Can I send you a 90-second video of it working in my daughter's salon?" Only salons that say yes get the video. That yes is their permission for you to keep talking to them, which is what POPIA asks for.
