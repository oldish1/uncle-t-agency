# What we need to build the best T & T Engineering site in Cape Town

The goal: a site that looks built by a senior web professional, wins the quote, and becomes Uncle T Agency's showcase.

## A. Facts from T & T (never invented, each one goes on the site as stated)
1. Legal name and trading name (T & T Engineering or T & T Jeftha Engineering).
2. Year founded (2001) and Errol Jeftha's story in his own words: two or three sentences.
3. Certificates: ISO, B-BBEE level, any safety or welding approvals. Ask for copies.
4. Laser: maximum steel thickness, bed size, materials it cuts (mild steel, stainless, aluminium, brass).
5. Press brake: maximum thickness, tooling, longest part, accuracy.
6. CNC punch: sheet size and what it makes best.
7. Other capability: rolling, welding, turning, milling, copper pot stills, Supra machines. What is the largest job they have done?
8. Typical lead time, minimum order, whether they deliver or customers collect.
9. Opening hours (we have Mon to Thu 07:30 to 16:30, Fri 07:30 to 13:30). Weekend and public holiday?
10. Phone numbers: which is for quotes? WhatsApp number confirmed. Email for quotes.
11. Industries and the work they want more of (distilleries, meat processing, others).
12. Three to five customers who agree to be named, and two short quotes with permission.
13. What do they want customers to do first: request a quote, call, or send drawings?

## B. Pictures and film (this is what makes it look expensive)
Shoot on a phone in landscape, 4K, steady, in good light. About 90 minutes at the workshop.
1. The laser cutting: low angle, close on sparks, 10 to 15 seconds. Also a wide shot of the whole machine.
2. The press brake bending a long sheet, from the side, ram coming down, 10 to 15 seconds. Wide shot of the machine.
3. The CNC punch running.
4. Errol at work or at the front of the workshop, and the team. Ask permission for each person.
5. Finished parts laid out on a clean bench or pallet, close and wide.
6. The copper pot stills and Supra machines.
7. The building outside, the sign, the street (Windsor Road, Lansdowne).
8. The inside of the workshop, wide.
Safety: hard hats and eye protection where the work needs them, no one standing in a danger zone.

## C. Business and access
1. Who owns the domain ttengineering.co.za and who hosts the old site? We need their help (or a DNS login) to point the domain at the new site.
2. Business email addresses and who reads them.
3. Google Business Profile for the workshop (address, hours, photos). Free and very important for "laser cutting Cape Town" searches.
4. A decision on the quote tool: email only, or a WhatsApp assistant for after hours (later phase).
5. Who approves the site, and a date for launch.
6. Which accounting software they use (for the later leak and invoicing work).

## D. What Claude and Theo build in
- Fast loading on a cheap phone and bad data (compressed film, lazy loading).
- Local search work: titles, descriptions and structured business data for Lansdowne and Cape Town, a page for each service (laser cutting, press brake bending, CNC punching).
- A proof strip, industries section, spec cards, a real FAQ, a gallery of finished work, and a company profile PDF.
- WhatsApp and call buttons that work on every page, a quote form with a drawing upload, and thank-you messages.
- Privacy notice (POPIA) and cookie notice, plus simple visit analytics.
- Tested on phones, tablets and laptops, with keyboard use and reduced-motion settings.
- Hosting on Netlify on their own domain, with Theo able to update it later.

## E. For the showcase (Uncle T Agency)
- A small "Built by Uncle T Agency" credit.
- Before and after of the site, plus the result numbers (quote requests, calls) after 30 days.
- A short testimonial from Errol or his son, with permission.

## Rule
Only publish facts, numbers, names, photographs and quotes that T & T has given us in writing or by message.
