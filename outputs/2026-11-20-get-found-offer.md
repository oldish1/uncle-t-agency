# "Get Found": turning the CHALES work into a product

> Idea and plan, not a live offer yet. Based on the reel by @champmack (Instagram, 19 Nov 2026), what we actually did for CHALES this week, and the pricing already set on 16 Nov (`context/numbers.md`).

## What the reel is, and whether it works

The reel says: type `/seo` into Claude, paste your website, and it shows why Google isn't sending customers, ranks the fixes biggest first, and can make the fixes itself (page titles, descriptions, missing pages). It's the same idea as the claude-seo repo we already used, and the audit side genuinely works. On CHALES it found real problems in about 20 minutes: no analytics, a fake map ID, no sitemap, one page for every service, mismatched phone numbers and hours.

What the reel leaves out:
- **It doesn't rank anyone.** An audit is a to-do list. A salon wins on Google Maps through a complete profile, reviews and being nearby, and no SEO skill does that for you.
- **No real ranking data.** Where she ranks and what people search needs Search Console or a paid data tool.
- **The "$15,000 a month" line is a hook.** The post asks people to comment for a link, which is how the author feeds an audience for courses. Don't copy that language.

So: the tool works as an audit and fix machine. The business value is in everything around it.

## Can we sell it? Yes, if we sell the result and the work, not the tool

Most small Cape Town businesses have a half-empty Google listing, no reviews and a one-page website. They don't know AI tools now answer "who does keratin in Bellville?" either. Almost nobody local is selling that, and you have a live case study (CHALES) and a booking system (Lexi) that turns found customers into bookings. Competitors sell visibility or booking. You'd sell both together.

**The pitch in one line:** "I get you found on Google Maps, Google and AI tools like ChatGPT and Claude, and Lexi books them on WhatsApp."

## The offer ladder

| Step | What | Price (suggestion, test it) |

| 1. Found Check | A one-page report on their website and Google listing: 3 biggest problems, what a competitor does better, one fix they can do today. Sent on WhatsApp. | Free. 15 to 20 minutes each |
| 2. Get Found | Google profile completed, service pages with real prices and FAQs, schema, sitemap, Search Console, Bing and Apple listings, review QR and the "how did we do" page | R1,499 setup (matches all plans), then about R599 a month for weekly Google post, review replies, monthly report, page refresh |
| 3. Full machine | Lexi + website + Get Found | Needs pricing once you have 2 or 3 real clients, I'd guess near R1,300 a month after R1,499 setup |

The monthly fee is what makes it a business, because ranking takes months and reviews keep coming in. Check these numbers against your costs (Apify or other data tools) before you quote.

## Rules so it doesn't bite you

1. **Never promise a position or a date.** Promise the work, a monthly report with real numbers, and what you'll fix next. If a client wants "guaranteed page one", walk away.
2. **No unproven upsell** (your own rule in `context/offer.md`). CHALES numbers, reviews, Maps position and bookings from Google, are the proof, due around end of January. Until then, sell the free Found Check and the Lexi founding five, and only quote Get Found to those five at the founding rate.
3. **Protect your time.** Day job. Each Get Found client costs roughly 3 hours a month once set up. Cap it at 5 clients until the monthly report is automated.
4. **Stay inside Google's rules.** No fake reviews, no review-for-discount, no hiding the Google button from unhappy clients.
5. **Don't undersell Lexi.** Lexi is still the flagship and the first paying client is the priority. Found is what you put in the free check to open the door.
