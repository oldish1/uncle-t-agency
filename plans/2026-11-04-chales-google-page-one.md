## Phase 2: The reviews machine and the rebooking machine (weeks 2–8, Nov to Dec)

This is the part from the reel: the machine behind the website that gets customers and keeps them coming back. CHALES is where we build and prove it before selling it to anyone else (the rule already set in `context/offer.md`).

### Reviews machine
1. **This week, by hand:** Chanté WhatsApps her last 30 to 50 clients personally with the direct review link. A personal message from her will get far more reviews than any automation. Aim for 10 in the first two weeks.
2. **Every visit, in person:** at the end of the appointment, "If you're happy, would you leave me a Google review? It really helps." A small card or sticker at the mirror with a QR code to the review link.
3. **Automatic, through Lexi:** the evening after each appointment, Lexi sends a short thank-you with the review link. *(Claude builds this in Make. Messages sent outside the 24-hour chat window need a Meta-approved template, which carries a small per-message fee from Meta.)*
4. **Reply to every review** within a day or two, mentioning the treatment and Bellville naturally ("Thanks Lauren, glad you loved your nanoplastia!").
5. **Never** pay for reviews, buy them, or give discounts in exchange for them. Google bans it and can wipe all her reviews or suspend the listing.

**Target:** 15 reviews by mid-December, 30+ by end of January, 50+ by end of March.

### Rebooking machine
1. **Book the next one before she leaves.** When Lexi confirms a booking, or the day after the visit, Lexi offers the next appointment at the right gap for that treatment, for example:
   - Wash & blow dry: weekly (her membership customers)
   - Root touch-up: about 6 weeks
   - Cut and colour: about 6 to 8 weeks
   - Keratin, Brazilian, Nanoplastia: about 3 months

   *Chanté confirms the right gaps for her clients; these are starting guesses.*
2. **"It's time" reminder.** If a client hasn't rebooked, Lexi messages when they're due: "Hi Thandi, it's been 6 weeks since your root touch-up. Want me to book you in this week?" with open slots.
3. **Win-back.** Clients not seen in 60+ days get one comeback message with a reason to return (a named special, the membership offer). Same approach as her best TikTok posts: a named offer plus a reason to act now.
4. **The client list behind it.** Lexi's tracking sheet already records bookings from Lexi. Chanté's older clients from before Lexi need adding: name, number, last visit, last treatment. Claude can help turn her WhatsApp chats into that list.

**Target:** by end of January, at least 4 in 10 clients book their next visit before leaving or from the reminder. Track it monthly in the sheet.

3. **TikTok captions name the place.** Put "Oakdale, Bellville" and the treatment in every caption. Her video already ranks number 5 on Google; more of those help.
4. **One new helpful page a month** on the website answering a real question (prices, aftercare, "nanoplastia vs keratin").
5. **Local mentions.** Get her name and link onto local pages: Bellville and Oakdale community Facebook groups, a collab with a nearby nail tech or makeup artist, a school or church fundraiser voucher. Each one tells Google she's a real local business.
