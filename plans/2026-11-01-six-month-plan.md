## What the video actually says (and what to ignore)

Take these:

1. **Reps beat theory.** Nate got good by building and selling dozens of times. The first 5 to 10 jobs are rough for everyone. Expect it, don't read it as failure.
2. **Talk to buyers.** His first sales calls taught him how to explain value without scaring people. Every salon owner you talk to sharpens the pitch.
3. **Experience over revenue early.** Founding clients at a discount for a testimonial and a number is right. A big one-off job that eats months is a trap.
4. **Do more of what's working.** He kept saying block the noise. There's a new shiny AI thing every week. Ignore it.
5. **One ICP, tunnel vision.** Salons in the Cape Flats suburbs. Not "any business".
6. **Less is more.** He cut his offer to two courses and his calls from five to one a week. Lexi plus a website is already enough product.
7. **Data is king.** One tracked number per client (no-shows down, after-hours bookings caught) is the sales proof for the next client.
8. **Under-promise.** Screen clients who think AI is magic.
9. **Content brings leads.** Under 500 subscribers, he still got a few inbound leads a week. Authenticity plus consistency.

Leave these:

- His $300K is mostly a paid community ($200K). The agency side is about $100K. Don't copy the YouTube-educator route.
- The video ends in a sales pitch for Liam's accelerator. Don't buy anything off the back of it.
- Your buyers are on WhatsApp, TikTok and walking past your car, not on YouTube.

## The math (so the target is real)

- R499/month each. 10 clients is R4,990 MRR. 20 clients is R9,980 MRR. Setup fees at R1,499 are extra, once.
- To leave the day job you need a number. Put it in `private/numbers-detail.md` (take-home per month). Quit rule: MRR covers 100% of that for 2 months in a row, plus 3 months of savings. Not before.
- That likely means 20 to 30 clients, or a higher-priced second product. Both are normal for month 7 to 12, so the six-month goal below is the runway, not the finish.

## Month 2 to 3 (Dec, Jan): prove and repeat

- Every client gets a 30-day check-in with one number written down (bookings after hours, no-shows before and after).
- Ask every happy client for 2 referrals. Offer one free month for each referral that pays.
- Collect 3 short voice or video testimonials.
- Fix onboarding so one client takes under a day, not 4 (use `plans/client-template.md`).
- Build upsells (reviews, reactivation) only after a client asks for it twice.

## Month 4 to 5 (Feb, Mar): add leverage

- Hire or partner for the repetitive part. Candidates: Chante or Nita for onboarding messages, a part-time helper for Saturday visits, paid per client closed.
- Test one second vertical with 10 conversations. If it closes at the same rate as hair salons, keep it. If not, drop it.
- Revisit pricing: if close rate is above 50%, raise the setup fee for new clients.
