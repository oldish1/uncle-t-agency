# Launch and traffic plan: step by step (tick as you go)

Goal: website live next week, then a steady flow of the right owners messaging you on WhatsApp, then paying clients.
Rule: warm first, cold second, content third, ads last (Hormozi's order, adapted for local owners).
Research behind it: `reference/research/2026-11-03-traffic-pricing-voice.md`. Who does each step: **T** = Theo, **C** = Claude.

## PHASE 2: Weeks 2 to 4 (build the habit)

Every week:
21. [ ] **T** 25 personal messages to salon and shop owners (cold but personal, one by one).
22. [ ] **T** 5 demos: let them try Lexi on their own phone, or show her live.
23. [ ] **T** At least 3 posts, aiming for one a day. Document, don't create: a clip of a demo, a missed-call story, a Chales booking, a tip. **C** cuts the clips.
24. [ ] **T** Post a useful tip in 2 local Facebook or WhatsApp groups. No hard selling.
25. [ ] **C** Write the week's messages, captions and one new reel.
26. [ ] **T+C** Friday review: messages sent, replies, demos, yeses, WhatsApp taps. Do more of what worked.

Once, in these weeks:
27. [ ] **T** Make the founding offer simple (the strategy says five founding clients at a discount for a testimonial and one tracked number). Write it in one sentence.
28. [ ] **T** Sign the first client. **C** builds their Lexi from the template, **T** tests it with them.
29. [ ] **T+C** After 2 weeks, collect their result and a short quote (with permission). Make it a post and add it to the site. Only real quotes and real numbers.

## VOICE track (starts once the first paying client is live)

V1. [ ] **C** Pick a voice platform to test (Vapi or Retell have free test credit) and connect it to the demo number, keeping "Allow voice calls" off until it works.
V2. [ ] **T+C** 20 test calls in English and Afrikaans with different accents. Write down how often it books correctly and how fast it answers.
V3. [ ] **T+C** Work out real cost per call from the test. Set the add-on price (about R500 to R900 a month is the starting guess).
V4. [ ] **T** Offer it first to one existing client as a pilot. Then add "Lexi answers chats and calls" to the site and prices.
Keep voice honest on the site until it exists ("coming soon" is already there).

## WEEKLY NUMBERS (write them down every Friday)

| Number | Starting target (an estimate, adjust after week 2) |
|---|---|
| Personal messages sent | 25 a week |
| Replies | 5 to 8 |
| Demos | 3 to 5 |
| Site visits | 100 to 150 a week at first |
| WhatsApp taps from the site | 10 a week |
| Clients signed | 1 or 2 a month at first |

Traffic is only useful if it turns into chats. The WhatsApp taps and demos matter more than visits.
