# Website traffic plan: uncletsautomation.co.za

Written 10 Nov 2026. Goal: turn visitors into WhatsApp messages to Theo, and get five founding clients. Visitors are not the goal, conversations are.

## The honest picture

Our buyers are solo Cape Town salon owners. They live on WhatsApp and Instagram, not on Google. So the website is a **trust page** that we send people to, more than a page people find. Google listing helps over months, not this week. The fast traffic comes from our own messages, videos and the Chales link.

## Step 0: done today

- Removed the "hidden from Google" setting and deployed (10 Nov). Sitemap and robots were already fine.
- Lexi price fixed to R1,499 setup and R499 a month.

## Step 1: tell Google (30 minutes, this week, Theo)

1. Google Search Console: add uncletsautomation.co.za as a domain property. Verify (domains.co.za DNS TXT record, or the Netlify option if offered).
2. Submit `https://uncletsautomation.co.za/sitemap.xml`.
3. URL inspection: paste the home page, click Request indexing.
4. Check back in a week. A new site often takes days to a few weeks to appear.

## Step 3: make the page convert

- Every plan button opens WhatsApp with the prefilled message (already built). Keep it.
- The first screen shows the price and "live at a Bellville salon". Keep that proof.
- Add real Chales results and a quote once Chante's reviews exist. No invented reviews.
- Review QR poster at Chales (needs Chante's review link) feeds her Google rating, which also helps our proof.

## Step 4: measure (Theo said the tracker is set up)

Look once a week at: visitors, where they came from, clicks on the WhatsApp buttons, new WhatsApp chats, replies from salons. Write the four numbers in the ledger on Fridays.

Targets for the first 30 days (a guide, not a promise): 200 visitors, 15 WhatsApp chats from the site, 5 conversations that reach a demo, 1 to 3 founding clients.

## Do not

- Pay for backlinks or SEO packages.
- Promise page-one Google results.
- Message salons that said no.
- Quote website pricing as final until Theo confirms it.
