# Plan: the agent team, built for making money

Date: 26 Oct 2026. Source: Morgan DeBaun on The Calum Johnson Show, "The Mom Who Mastered AI Agents" (youtu.be/2ulXtLUDvdU). What we take from it, and what we don't.

## What she does that matters to us

1. **A folder is the headquarters.** One folder holds the business context. Every agent starts by reading it. Ours is this workspace.
2. **First agent is the Chief Revenue Officer.** Its only job is money: a revenue target, her data, and activities it does on her behalf (find the right people, message them, set a reminder to follow up).
3. **Three first agents:** revenue, inbox (sorts email, flags opportunities, drafts replies), marketing (watches competitors and trends, sends a weekly report).
4. **Every agent has a goal tied to revenue**, and reports "problem, what I did, the result", not just the problem.
5. **Rules before autonomy.** No email, payment or publishing without her approval. A checker agent tests every output against the rules before she sees it.
6. **Runs in the cloud**, so it works while her computer is off, and reports to her where she already is (email, WhatsApp).
7. **Start with 30 minutes saved a day**, not a 40-agent empire.

## Our team (four, not forty)

| Agent | Job | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Rex (chief of staff) | One WhatsApp message each morning: today's 5 leads, replies waiting, bookings, money. Takes orders from Theo and hands them to the others. | Live on the test number. Real number waits on Meta verification, which waits on uncletsautomation.co.za. |
| Revenue (Scout + outreach-writer) | Finds salons with no booking page, drafts one tailored WhatsApp message each, fills the Pipeline Sheet. | Live. |
| Reply Helper (inbox) | Drafts the answer when a salon replies. Flags "interested" fast. | Designed, not built. |
| Checker (rules) | Blocks anything with an invented claim, a missing opt-out line, or a number marked do-not-contact. | Rules written in the outreach-writer skill. Not a separate step yet. |

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- A human sends every first message from his own number. Agents draft, Theo sends.
- Nothing is published, paid or sent to a client without Theo's yes.
- Opt-outs are permanent. Never message a salon with a booking page we marked as skipped.
- No invented reviews, prices or results.

## The one target

Five founding clients (first month free, then R499 a month). Everything above exists to get there. Daily number: 5 messages sent. Weekly number: replies, demos booked, clients signed.

## Order of work

1. Outreach: record the Lexi video, send the 10 drafted messages, log who replied.
2. Daily brief: a scheduled morning run that checks the Sheet, drafts the next 5, and writes the day's list. Needs Theo's yes.
3. Reply Helper: built the first time someone replies.
4. Agency website (uncletsautomation.co.za) so Meta verification can pass and Rex moves to the real SIM.
5. After 10 clients: multi-stylist and the extra Lexi features (`plans/2026-10-26-multi-stylist.md`).

## What we do not copy

Advisor agents that imitate famous people, blog repurposing, a family dashboard. Not what a salon-booking business needs this month.
